# Lailak: a banker review we still need
Prepared 10 November 2026. No interview, bank pilot or customer endorsement has been completed.

## Proposed reviewer
A construction-loan monitoring officer or technical inspector, plus the person who owns the process and purchase budget. SQB and Ipoteka Bank publish construction products with progress evidence and tranche conditions:
https://www.ipotekabank.uz/ru/corporate/crediting/escrow-loan/
https://sqb.uz/business/small-business-credits/qurilish-uchun-ru/

## 30-minute process review
1. Walk through the last construction tranche approved or held back.
2. Show a redacted request, work-completion record, supporting invoices and the last inspection.
3. Identify what was retyped, compared, questioned or missing.
4. Explain current software and the source of the authoritative status.
5. Review our synthetic packet and identify a useful or useless finding.
6. Name the person who can approve a historical evaluation and any paid next step.

## Proposed pilot
Use approved historical cases, including clean and difficult cases. Freeze checks before seeing outcomes. Keep synthetic development examples outside the evaluation.
Compare preparation + review + correction time with and without Lailak. Count confirmed issues, false flags, missed issues and additional work.
Add satellite context separately to measure its incremental value.
Target 30% less total review time, subject to agreement. This is a target, not an achieved saving.
A pilot of 20–30 files assesses workflow feasibility; it cannot establish precise fraud/default performance.

## Stop or narrow
After 8–12 relevant interviews, if nobody describes a repeated problem, gives an approved example or sponsors a trial, stop adding features and revisit the customer task.

## Message draft for the founder to send
We are building Lailak to help review construction-loan funding requests. Could we walk through one redacted past request with someone who checks supporting documents and construction progress? We would like to understand the current process and show a short synthetic example. We are seeking feedback first, not claiming the system is ready for lending decisions.
